Checklist Overview

Required Records for Landlords & Tenants

According to Maharashtra e-registration guidelines, valid identity and ownership records are mandatory.

Owner / Landlord Requirements Mandatory
  • Original Aadhaar Card Mandatory for live biometric fingerprint scanning. Both sides should be legible.
  • PAN Card Copy Self-attested copy required for financial identity and statutory tax records.
  • Ownership Proof Any one of: Property Index II document, electricity bill (under owner's name), or property tax receipt.
  • Passport Size Photograph Recent passport-size photograph (white/light background) for document embossing.
Tenant Requirements Mandatory
  • Original Aadhaar Card Mandatory for live biometric fingerprint verification during the doorstep visit.
  • PAN Card Copy Official taxpayer identification copy to match with Aadhaar records.
  • Permanent Address Proof / Corporate ID Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or corporate employment badge copy.
  • Passport Size Photograph Recent color passport-size photograph for lease deed registration records.
Helpful Guidelines

Important Verification Guidelines

Ensure a trouble-free registration by noting these key legal specifics.

Original Aadhaar Required

During the biometric executive's visit, both parties must present original Aadhaar cards to match biometric hash credentials.

Witness Verification

Two witnesses with valid Aadhaar numbers are required by the e-registration portal. Our team assists with witness documentation.

Rapid 24-Hour Approval

Once biometric scanning is completed for all parties, government approval usually concludes in 24 to 48 working hours.
