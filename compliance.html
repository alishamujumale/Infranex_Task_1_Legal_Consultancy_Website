Section 55 Compliance

Under Section 55 of Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, it is mandatory for landlords to register leave and license agreements in writing. Failure to do so carries penalties and legal risks for property owners.

Police Intimation Submission

We assist with police intimation filing for tenant verification, ensuring complete safety and compliance for residential premises.

Government Stamp Duty Payment

Stamp duty is calculated accurately based on monthly rent, deposit amount, and duration, paid directly through GRAS (Government Receipt Accounting System).
