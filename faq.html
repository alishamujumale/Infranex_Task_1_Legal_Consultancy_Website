Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to questions regarding legal enforceability, doorstep biometrics, required documents, and government turnaround timelines.

Is an online rent agreement legally valid in court?

Yes. Agreements registered through the government's official e-registration portal with UIDAI Aadhaar biometric authentication are 100% legally binding, fully compliant with Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, and admissible as conclusive evidence in all courts of law.

How long does the registration process take?

Once biometric scanning is completed by both landlord and tenant, the file is transmitted to the Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR). Government approval and certificate generation typically concludes in 24 to 48 working hours.

Do I need to visit the Sub-Registrar office?

No. Our authorized field executive visits your home or office with a government-approved biometric fingerprint scanner. You do not need to stand in long queues or visit any government building.

What documents do both parties need to provide?

Both parties require original Aadhaar cards (mandatory for live biometric verification), PAN card copies, and passport-size photographs. The landlord additionally provides property ownership proof (Index II copy or electricity bill).

Can I cancel or modify my agreement after registration?

A registered agreement cannot be modified directly once stamped. However, with mutual consent between both parties, a Deed of Cancellation can be registered. If terms change, a fresh registered addendum or new agreement can be executed.

Is stamp duty included in the service charges?

Our package price covers drafting, identity verification, doorstep executive visit, biometric processing, and courier dispatch. Government stamp duty and registration fees are calculated separately based on the standard IGR formula and paid directly to the government treasury.

Do you provide service outside Pune?

Yes. While our headquarters are in Shivaji Nagar, Pune, we provide doorstep biometric service across Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and remote e-registration services throughout Maharashtra (Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Nashik, and Nagpur).

What if one party is out of the city or state?

We provide multi-location executive appointments where our representative meets the landlord in one city and the tenant in another, or facilitate remote Aadhaar OTP-based digital execution where permitted.
