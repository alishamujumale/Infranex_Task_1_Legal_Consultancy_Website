Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about registration, stamp duty, biometrics, and validity.

1. Is e-Registration legally valid across India?

Yes. Digital leave & license agreements registered through official state portals with Aadhaar biometric authentication hold 100% legal validity.

2. What if the landlord or tenant is outside Pune / Maharashtra?

We offer remote e-signature and out-of-station biometric assistance so both parties can complete registration regardless of their location.

3. What is the standard duration for a residential rent agreement?

The most common period is 11 months, but agreements can also be registered for 22, 33, or up to 60 months as needed.

4. How do I track my government registration status?

We provide official government tracking numbers (MH-REG / IGR token number) so you can monitor status directly online.
