Statutory Compliance

Legal & Regulatory Compliance Framework

Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, every tenancy agreement must be in writing and officially registered. We guarantee 100% statutory adherence to all government sub-registrar guidelines, biometric e-signatures under the IT Act 2000, and complete admissibility in court under Section 49 of the Indian Registration Act 1908.

Section 55 Maharashtra Rent Control Act

Mandatory legal registration protecting landlords and tenants from penalties and unregularized tenancies.

Indian Registration Act 1908 (Section 49)

Carries absolute legal evidentiary weight admissible before all judicial and civil court authorities in India.

Information Technology Act 2000

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online rent agreement legally valid in court?

Yes. Agreements registered via the official government portal with Aadhaar biometric verification are 100% legally binding and admissible in any court of law.

How long does the registration process take?

Once biometric verification is completed by both landlord and tenant, government approval and registration certificate generation typically takes 24 to 48 working hours.

Do I need to visit the Sub-Registrar office?

No. Our doorstep executive visits your location with an authorized biometric scanner so you never need to wait in government office lines.

Can I cancel or modify my agreement later?

A registered agreement cannot be altered directly, but can be officially terminated via a registered deed of cancellation with mutual consent.
