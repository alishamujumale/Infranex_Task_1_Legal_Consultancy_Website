Legal & Regulatory Standard

Fully Compliant With Registration Act

Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, every tenancy agreement must be in writing and registered with the Sub-Registrar. We adhere 100% to official procedures, ensuring peace of mind for both property owners and occupants.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online rent agreement legally valid in court?

Yes. Agreements registered via the official e-Registration portal with Aadhaar biometric verification are 100% legally binding and admissible in court.

How long does the registration process take?

Once biometric verification is completed by both landlord and tenant, government approval usually takes 24 to 48 working hours.

Do I need to visit the Sub-Registrar office?

No. With our doorstep biometric service, our representative visits your location so you don't have to wait in government office queues.
