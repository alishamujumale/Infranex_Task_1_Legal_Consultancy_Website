Streamlined 4-Step Flow

How It Works

A simple, hassle-free procedure to draft, verify, and register your rent agreement without ever stepping out of your house.

The Process

Four Steps to Legal Registration

We manage drafting, verification, government biometrics, and final delivery on your behalf.

01

Enter Details

Submit the landlord, tenant, property address, and financial terms via our secure online portal or through a consultant.

02

Approve Draft

Our legal team generates the official draft and shares it with you via email or WhatsApp for verification and clause approval.

03

Biometrics & E-Sign

Our field executive arrives at your doorstep for Aadhaar biometric verification, or you can complete an instant remote e-sign.

04

Receive Agreement

The Sub-Registrar approves the submission, and your digitally stamped, legally registered agreement is delivered.

Step-by-Step Breakdown

What Happens at Each Stage

01

Information Intake & Document Collection

You fill in our simple form or send your details over WhatsApp. We collect basic details: owner information, tenant identity, monthly rent, security deposit amount, and agreement duration.

Estimated time: 10 minutes
02

Legal Review & Draft Generation

Our documentation specialists prepare a customized draft incorporating all statutory covenants under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act. Both parties can review and request edits if required.

Estimated time: 1 to 2 hours
03

Aadhaar Biometric Verification at Doorstep

A certified executive visits your home or office with a UIDAI-approved biometric fingerprint scanner. Both parties provide biometric verification with their Aadhaar cards without any physical paperwork.

Doorstep appointment: As per your preferred slot
04

Government Approval & Registration

The e-Registration file is submitted to the government Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR Maharashtra). Upon verification by the Sub-Registrar, the official registered certificate is issued.

Final delivery: Within 24-48 working hours
